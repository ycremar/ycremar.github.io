[CHAOS]
🚫 | No Volleys | Everyone stays behind the service line. Let it bounce.
🫲 | Weak Hand Only | Hit every shot with your non-dominant hand.
🌈 | Lob Only | Every shot must be a lob.
🔇 | Silent Game | No talking. Not even the score.
🪞 | Mirror Partner | You and your partner must hit the same type of shot (both forehand or both backhand).
🧱 | Forehand Only | Backhands are banned. Run around them.
🤝 | Two bounces | Ball can bounce twice before you hit it.
🏃 | Switch Sides | Partners swap sides after every shot.
⚡ | Volley Only | Hit every ball out of the air. No bounces.
🔄 | Alternate Hits | You and your partner must take turns hitting each shot.
📏 | Short Court | Only the service boxes are in. Deep shots are out.
