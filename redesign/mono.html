about

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University, advised by Prof. Shuiwang Ji, and my B.S. in Statistics from the School of the Gifted Young at USTC. My research improves the applicability and reliability of machine learning to drive industrial applications and scientific discovery (AI4Science). I serve as an Area Chair for Language and Molecule @ ACL and Scaling Environments for Agents @ NeurIPS.

research

  • LLM post-training, reward modeling & RAG
  • Self-supervised learning & foundation models
  • Recommender systems
  • Model explainability & graph neural networks
  • AI4Science: geometric deep learning, bioinformatics, biomedical imaging
