About

Background

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University, advised by Prof. Shuiwang Ji. Before that, I obtained my B.S. in Statistics from the School of the Gifted Young at the University of Science and Technology of China. I currently serve as an Area Chair for Language and Molecule @ ACL and Scaling Environments for Agents @ NeurIPS, and on the program committees of several conferences.
